Privacy policy

Updated 16 November 2026

Your conversation history is not stored on Mayak servers.

Mayak is a client that connects to an XMPP server of your choice. We do not provide XMPP accounts or maintain our own server-side archive of your messages, photos or contacts. History is stored on your device and may also be stored by your chosen provider.

Notifications use a separate push service. It needs technical delivery data, not your conversation history. If you choose to contact support, we receive the contents of your request.

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Mayak app, its notification extension, the push.mayakapp.site push service and the Mayak website. For privacy questions and support, contact support@mayakapp.site.

Your XMPP provider, its file upload service, Apple and other services involved process data under their own policies. Mayak does not control their retention practices.

Data on your device

The app stores settings, account and contact addresses, message history and received attachments. Account passwords and encryption keys are stored in Keychain. The app and notification extension share local data on the same device to retrieve messages in the background. The Mayak push service does not need your account password or private OMEMO keys.

Local history is protected by iOS. OMEMO end-to-end encryption protects messages exchanged between devices; it does not mean that Mayak separately encrypts the entire local history database.

Messages and your chosen server

Mayak connects to your XMPP server to sign in, exchange messages and synchronise contacts and history. The server can access the technical information required for this: your account address, connection IP address, participant addresses, exchange times and presence information. Profiles, avatars and public device keys you publish also pass through the server.

When OMEMO is used, message content is encrypted for the participants’ devices. Exchange metadata remains visible to servers. Messages without end-to-end encryption may be accessible to the servers handling them.

Photos, Live Photos, videos, voice messages and files use your XMPP provider’s upload service. That service sees network information, transfer times and sizes. Content protection depends on attachment encryption and how the key is shared: a key in an unencrypted message is accessible to the messaging server. The provider determines retention of uploaded files and server archives.

Data needed for notifications

When you enable push, the app gives your XMPP server a delivery token issued by Apple. When a new event occurs, that server contacts push.mayakapp.site, which sends a signal through Apple Push Notification service (APNs).

  • The device token and delivery parameters route the notification to the appropriate app installation.
  • The sending server or component address and request metadata are used to process the request. Depending on the provider’s implementation, the request’s sender address may contain an account JID.
  • Connection information, delivery results and temporary per-token state support service operation, error handling and limits on repeated requests.

The push service builds a neutral new-message notification. It does not fetch your chat history. The extension retrieves the sender name and display text from your XMPP server and processes them on your device according to your preview settings.

This is technical data processing, not a fully anonymous exchange. Tokens and network information are processed during delivery even without logging. Request data supplied by your provider depends on its implementation.

You can disable notification display in iOS settings. This is not the same as deleting your provider’s push registration. Removing an account from Mayak starts deregistration; its outcome depends on server availability.

Calls and other connections

Calls use XMPP for signalling and WebRTC to protect audio and video transmission. STUN/TURN services help establish connections and receive network addresses. If your provider supplies no STUN server, the app uses Google’s stun.l.google.com service. A direct connection can reveal your IP address to your contact; having TURN does not itself guarantee that your address is hidden.

Link previews are off by default. If you enable them, the linked website receives a request from your device and sees its IP address. Place search uses Apple services; a location is shared with a contact at your request.

Permissions and diagnostics

The camera, microphone and photo library support media actions and calls you choose to make. Local network access supports call connections. Face ID supports app locking when enabled; Mayak does not receive biometric templates. You can change permissions in iOS settings.

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Sharing system reports with developers through Apple depends on your device settings. Notification diagnostics are kept locally; additional diagnostic logging in release builds is enabled manually. Technical logs should not be treated as fully anonymous. Review them before sharing them with support.

Support requests and reports

If you email support@mayakapp.site, we receive your reply address, message and any attachments you provide. This information is used to handle your request. We do not create a separate archive of support requests and delete processed messages from the working mailbox once the request is resolved. Deleting a message from the mailbox does not mean that the mail provider immediately deletes all copies.

The in-app report form shows a recipient and editable text, then opens an email draft. You decide whether to send it. If you select your XMPP provider’s contact, that provider receives the report, not Mayak. Conversations and logs are not attached automatically. Do not send passwords or encryption keys.

Retention and deletion

History and attachments stay locally until deleted. After migration from an earlier storage format, an older local history copy may remain. Deleting an individual message from active history does not necessarily remove it from that copy.

Removing an account from the device starts cleanup of its settings, keys, active and older history, and files established to belong to that account. Errors may leave cleanup incomplete. Exported files, backups and items saved to Photos must be deleted separately. Irrecoverable physical erasure of every trace on storage is not guaranteed.

Removing an account from the device does not delete it with your XMPP provider. A separate server-side deletion action is available if supported by the provider. Copies held by your contacts and the timing of server-side deletion are outside Mayak’s control.

For questions about access, correction or deletion of data you supplied to Mayak, contact support. Identify the request or service involved without sending passwords or secret keys. Contact your XMPP provider about its account and message archive. Applicable rights, including the right to contact a data protection authority, depend on the law in your country.

Website

The website is static. Its code uses no cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or external fonts. To serve a page, the hosting service receives your IP address and HTTP request information. The absence of page analytics does not mean that the host performs no data processing.
