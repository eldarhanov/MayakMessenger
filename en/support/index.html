Email support

Contact support about the app, suggestions or privacy requests. Account details and passwords with an XMPP provider are managed through that provider.

What to include

  • Your Mayak and iOS versions and device model.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened.
  • If useful, a screenshot with personal information removed.

Do not send passwords, encryption keys, tokens or conversations. Only share diagnostic logs if you choose to, after reviewing their contents.

Before contacting support

If sign-in fails, check your account address and your XMPP server’s availability. If notifications are missing, check Mayak’s notification permissions and Focus settings in iOS. Do not delete the app for troubleshooting without a verified history backup.
